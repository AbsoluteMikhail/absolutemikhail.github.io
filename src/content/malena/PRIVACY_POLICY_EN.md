## 3. Data the Bot may receive

### 3.1. Telegram data

When you interact with the Bot, Telegram may provide:

- the numeric Telegram ID of the user and chat;
- first name, last name, and username (`@username`), if available;
- message text and file captions;
- message date and number, and reply information;
- chat type and available group or channel information;
- photos, documents, audio, video, and other attachments sent to the Bot;
- commands, button presses, reactions, and other actions within the Bot;
- information needed to check subscriptions, perform moderation, handle join requests, or send proactive messages.

This information may be personal data even when published in an open or group chat. The Bot receives information through Telegram only to the extent available under the settings of the relevant chat and required for enabled features.

### 3.2. Information you provide

The Bot receives messages and files that you send directly, publish in a group available to it, or ask it to process.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment information, identity documents, medical information, or other confidential or sensitive information.

The Bot is not intended to process special categories of personal data and is configured not to use or retain information about health, racial or ethnic origin, political, religious or philosophical beliefs, or intimate life. If such information appears in a message, the Bot must refuse to process it. Automated detection can make mistakes, so users must not send this information to the Bot.

### 3.3. User memory

To make conversations coherent and personal, the Bot may retain:

- recent private conversation history;
- short ordinary facts such as a name, interests, profession, or hobbies;
- facts that you explicitly ask it to remember.

The Bot is configured not to retain phone numbers, email or postal addresses, passwords, tokens, payment information, health information, debts, political or religious views, intimate-life information, or account links in long-term memory. Automated checks cannot guarantee perfect detection, so this information must not be sent to the Bot.

### 3.4. Group-chat data

In groups, the Bot may process:

- no more than the 10 latest messages for current conversational context;
- messages from the last 24 hours, with the available author name and message number, to create a brief daily conversation summary; the summary is not used to create long-term participant profiles;
- information about the latest media item, without permanently storing the file, to handle requests such as “look at the previous photo”;
- messages needed for moderation, reactions, replies, and abuse prevention.

Messages available to the Bot may be temporarily processed without a direct request when a group administrator has enabled context, summary, or moderation features. Group administrators should notify participants that the Bot is present and explain its enabled features.

### 3.5. Proactive messages and usage statistics

In private chats, the Bot may retain Telegram ID, display name, `@username`, subscription status, and interaction dates. This allows the Bot occasionally to start a conversation with users who previously opened a private chat with it and have not disabled proactive messages.

Proactive messages are intended for conversation with the Bot and are not advertising. Users can disable them at any time with `/unsubscribe` and re-enable them with `/subscribe`. If their purpose changes or advertising is introduced, the Developer will update the terms and request prior consent where required.

For operation and security, the Bot may also retain technical records including feature calls, delivery results, errors, user, chat and message numbers, event time, and limited request fragments. Secrets are masked where reasonably possible.

The Bot does not sell user data or use it to build advertising profiles.

## 4. Purposes of processing

Data is processed only to the extent needed to:

- respond to messages and maintain conversational context;
- perform commands and user requests;
- analyse supported images, documents, audio, and other content;
- generate or edit text, images, or video;
- remember permitted user facts;
- create group summaries;
- enforce limits and prevent spam or abuse;
- moderate groups and handle join requests;
- send non-advertising proactive messages to eligible users;
- diagnose errors, protect security, and improve stability;
- comply with legal requirements and protect lawful rights.

Processing is performed with the user’s consent expressed by starting or continuing interaction after receiving a link to this Policy, or on another applicable legal basis where provided by law. Users may stop optional processing with the available commands or by contacting the Developer.

## 5. Artificial intelligence and third-party services

The Bot may use external services:

- **Telegram** for messaging and the Bot interface;
- **Google Gemini** for text and file processing, information retrieval, image generation, and certain video functions;
- **GigaChat** for text and supported-file processing and image generation;
- Russian hosting infrastructure and Redis storage for temporary context, limits, and service data.

Depending on the selected mode, the relevant AI service may receive the request text, necessary conversation context, an available user name for response personalisation, and attached files. The numeric Telegram user ID is not sent to AI services. Certain background functions may use Google Gemini even when normal conversation is handled by another AI service.

Third parties process supplied data under their own policies:

- [Telegram Privacy Policy](https://telegram.org/privacy);
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy);
- [GigaChat / Sber Personal Data Policy](https://www.sberbank.com/ru/personal_policy).

The Developer does not determine every internal retention period or operation performed by these services. Do not submit confidential or sensitive information. Using a feature that requires AI processing means the relevant request content may be sent to that provider to perform the feature.

## 6. Retention periods

- recent private conversation history: up to **3 hours**;
- current group context: no more than the **10 latest messages**, with older messages replaced by newer ones;
- information about the latest group media item: up to **10 minutes**; the file itself is downloaded for analysis only after a relevant request;
- group log for a daily summary: messages no older than **24 hours**;
- completed daily summary: until the next summary is created or the feature is disabled;
- request counters and temporary restrictions: normally until the end of the applicable hour or day;
- saved user facts: until deleted by the user or Developer;
- proactive-message subscription data: while needed to manage delivery and confirm opt-out;
- technical logs and delivery reports: no more than **7 days**, with newer records cyclically replacing older ones.

The Developer does not create backups of user memory, conversation history, group context, or daily summaries. Their loss does not prevent further use of the Bot but may temporarily reduce response continuity and personalisation.

Some information may be retained longer where required by law or necessary to investigate abuse, resolve a dispute, or protect lawful rights.

## 7. Disclosure and international processing

The Developer does not sell personal data.

Data may be disclosed to:

- Telegram and the selected AI service to perform a Bot function;
- server infrastructure providers to the extent required to operate the Bot;
- public authorities where required by law.

If ownership of the Bot changes, the Developer will delete or anonymise user memory, recent conversation history, and the proactive-message subscription register under the Developer’s control, unless retention is required by law or cannot technically be stopped. This does not cover data independently retained by Telegram, AI services, or other providers under their own rules.

External services may process data in other countries where the level of legal protection may differ from that in the user’s country.

## 8. Public messages

Messages in groups, comments, and channels are visible to other participants under Telegram settings. The Developer cannot make information confidential after a user has made it public.

When the Bot replies in a group, part of the original message or the meaning of the request may be visible to other participants. The Bot attempts to display saved personal facts through memory-management commands only in a private chat with the relevant user.

## 9. Managing your data

The Bot provides:

- `/privacy` — brief information about data and memory;
- `/memory` — view, add, or delete saved facts about yourself;
- `/forget_me` — delete saved user facts and recent private conversation history;
- `/unsubscribe` — disable proactive messages;
- `/subscribe` — re-enable proactive messages.

To correct a saved fact, open `/memory`, delete the incorrect item, and add the correct one. Memory should be managed in a private chat. If invoked in a group, the Bot will attempt to send the menu privately.

`/forget_me` does not delete Telegram messages, shared group messages or summaries, existing technical logs, delivery reports, limit counters, or data independently retained by third parties. For a broader access, correction, or deletion request, use the protected contact section at the end of this page.

Include your Telegram ID and the request details. To prevent fraudulent requests, the Developer may ask you to verify control of the account. Requests are handled without undue delay and within the time limits required by applicable law.

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, copy, correct, delete, or restrict your data, object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a competent data-protection authority. Withdrawal does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful.
